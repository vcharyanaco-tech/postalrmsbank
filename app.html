Mobile Banking App

Banking at your fingertips

The Postal RMS Bank mobile application brings convenient banking services to your smartphone. Access your accounts, check balances, and perform transactions on the go.


Features

  • Check account balances instantly
  • View transaction history
  • Download account statements
  • Locate nearby branches and ATMs
  • Access customer support

Download

The app is available for Android and iOS devices. Please visit your nearest branch or contact us for the download link and installation instructions.
